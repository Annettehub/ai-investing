China Internet: Who Pays for the AI Build?（中国互联网：谁为AI建设买单？）

核心摘要：配售不是价值毁灭，AI周期比预期更长但计算经济性仍具吸引力
On Monday, 24 Aug 2026, Alibaba raised HK$80bn (US$10.3bn) through 710m new shares at HK$112.70, an 8.4% discount to last Friday's close and 3.7% of existing shares. The Hong Kong shares fell 8.5% on 25 Aug 2026, versus Tencent down 2.2% and HSTECH down 2.1%. Only 0.3% of Alibaba's share price move is the arithmetic of issuing holders at a discount, in our view; we estimate the abnormal repricing of existing holders at about US$19bn, roughly twice the cash raised. Cost, in our view, Alibaba's sell-off is too large relative to the actual shareholder cost. Therefore, we believe the market is treating Alibaba's equity raise as evidence that China's AI capex cycle is becoming economically dangerous. We, on the other hand, argue that the more accurate conclusion is narrower: Alibaba's AI investment cycle is likely longer than expected, but the underlying compute economics remain attractive and the three CSPs in our coverage universe have very different funding risks. We maintain Overweight on Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu and we recommend investors accumulate Alibaba into the September-quarter print.
2026年8月24日周一，阿里巴巴以每股112.70港元发行7.1亿新股筹资800亿港元（约103亿美元），较上周五收盘价折让8.4%，占现有股份3.7%。8月25日港股下跌8.5%，同期腾讯跌2.2%，恒生科技指数跌2.1%。我们认为，阿里巴巴股价变动中仅0.3%来自折价发行的算术影响；估计现有股东的异常重定价约为190亿美元，约为筹资额的两倍。我们认为，阿里巴巴的抛售相对于实际股东成本而言过大。因此，我们认为市场将阿里巴巴的股权融资视为中国AI资本支出周期变得经济危险的信号。而我们认为更准确的结论更为狭义：阿里巴巴的AI投资周期可能比预期更长，但底层计算经济性仍然具有吸引力，我们覆盖范围内的三家云服务商具有截然不同的融资风险。我们维持阿里巴巴、腾讯和百度的"增持"评级，建议投资者在9月季报前逢低吸纳阿里巴巴。

三个核心判断
What changed is our interpretation of the AI investment cycle's duration, not our FY27 capex estimate or view of compute returns. We keep Alibaba FY27E capex at RMB200bn and treat the RMB270bn June-quarter run rate as a stress case. At RMB200bn capex, Alibaba's annual internal funding deficit is about RMB100bn, but the company has cRMB150bn of usable liquidity plus substantial debt capacity. So, liquidity or solvency is not the key risk. Four tests frame the transaction: the compute tranche is positive NPV on our vintage model (22% project IRR versus a cost of capital near 10%); EPS turns accretive from year two if at least 80% of proceeds become infrastructure but stays modestly dilutive at our 60% base mix; debt would have been about 3ppt better for EPS; and issuing below our HK$205 fair value reduces about 3ppt per existing share by about 1%. The placement therefore not free, but neither does it resemble the value destruction priced on Monday, in our view.
改变的是我们对AI投资周期持续时间的解读，而非我们对FY27资本支出的预测或对计算回报的看法。我们维持阿里巴巴FY27E资本支出为2000亿元人民币，并将6季度2700亿元人民币的运行速率视为压力情景。在2000亿元人民币资本支出下，阿里巴巴年度内部资金缺口约为1000亿元人民币，但公司拥有约1500亿元人民币的可用流动性加上充足的债务能力。因此，流动性或偿付能力不是关键风险。四个测试框定了这笔交易：计算部分在我们的vintage模型下NPV为正（22%的项目IRR对比约10%的资本成本）；如果至少80%的资金用于基建，EPS从第二年起开始增厚，但在我们60%的基准组合下仍有轻微稀释；债务对EPS的影响比股权好约3个百分点；以低于我们205港元合理价值发行，每股现有股份价值减少约1%。因此，这次配售并非没有代价，但在我们看来，它也不像周一市场定价所反映的价值毁灭。
Across the sector, three funding models are now visible. Alibaba uses parent equity plus debt after a rerating. Tencent funds capex from operating cash flow, bonds and a RMB875bn investment portfolio. Baidu borrows and seeks subsidiary equity through Kunlunxin. Each route has a shareholder cost - direct dilution at Alibaba, foregone portfolio returns and tax leakage at Tencent, and dilution of the AI asset at Baidu. We think the read-across from Alibaba's placement to Tencent and Baidu is overstated.
整个行业目前可见三种融资模式。阿里巴巴在评级调整后使用母公司股权加债务。腾讯从经营现金流、债券和8750亿元人民币投资组合中为资本支出提供资金。百度借款并通过昆仑万维寻求子公司股权。每条路径都有股东成本——阿里巴巴的直接稀释、腾讯的投资组合回报损失和税收流失、百度AI资产的稀释。我们认为，从阿里巴巴配售对腾讯和百度的外推影响被夸大了。
The key risks to our thesis are observable. Our Alibaba case weakens if mature ROIC on deployed proceeds falls below roughly 15%, the infrastructure share of AI spend approaches 40%, the three-year capex commitment rises materially above RMB380bn without a matching lift in external cloud growth, or compute pricing deflates as supply loosens.
我们论点的关键风险是可观察的。如果已部署资金的成熟ROIC降至约15%以下、AI支出中基建占比接近40%、三年资本承诺大幅超过3800亿元人民币而外部云增长未匹配提升、或计算定价随供给宽松而下降，我们对阿里巴巴的判断将被削弱。

Three funding models - and what each costs（三种融资模式及其成本）

同一战略判断，不同资产负债表：发行母公司股权的直觉法则是股价低于内在价值且项目IRR超过收益率时
The three companies are making the same strategic judgment on AI with different balance sheets and different share prices. The rule that falls out is intuitive: issue parent equity when the stock has rated and project IRR exceeds the earnings yield; recycle a portfolio when the portfolio is larger than the program; and raise at subsidiary level when the parent is cheap and the subsidiary is the AI asset. 'No parent equity' does not mean 'no cost'.
三家公司对AI做出相同的战略判断，但资产负债表和股价不同。得出的法则很直观：当股价低于内在价值且项目IRR超过收益率时发行母公司股权；当投资组合大于计划时回收投资组合；当母公司便宜且子公司是AI资产时在子公司层面融资。"不发行母公司股权"并不意味着"没有成本"。

Tencent's disposals surrender assets that we believe the market under-credits, so the case for recycling rests on the spread between roughly 20% capex ROIC and a high-single-digit portfolio return, less leakage. Baidu holders give up part of the one asset that carries the thesis. Alibaba's cost is the most visible and easiest to price - one reason the market priced it hardest.
腾讯的处置交出了我们认为市场低估的资产，因此回收的理由取决于约20%的资本支出ROIC与高个位数投资组合回报之间的利差，扣除流失。百度股东放弃了承载论点的那部分资产。阿里巴巴的成本最可见、最容易定价——这也是市场对其定价最严厉的原因之一。
Alibaba placement: four separate tests（阿里巴巴配售：四个独立测试）

四个不同问题：项目NPV、EPS增厚、股权vs债务、每股现有股东价值
Positive project NPV, EPS accretion, the choice of equity over debt, and value per existing share are different questions. Treating them as one is the main source of confusion in the placement debate, in our view.
正的项目NPV、EPS增厚、股权优于债务的选择、以及每股现有股东价值是不同的问题。在我们看来，将它们视为一个问题是配售辩论中混淆的主要来源。

Test 1：基建部分创造经济价值——22%项目IRR，2.9年回收期，NPV为正RMB36/每100元
Test 1 - Does the infrastructure create economic value? Yes, for the compute tranche. Our vintage model from our recently published note uses RMB1 of annual revenue per RMB1 of capex at full utilization, a 45% incremental EBITDA margin, 20% tax, a five-year straight-line economic life, and a 60% / 90% / 100% utilization ramp. Per RMB100 of capex, after-tax cash flows are (100) / 25.6 / 36.4 / 40.0 / 40.0 / 40.0, giving a 22% project IRR, 2.9-year payback and RMB36 NPV at a 10% discount rate. That reconciles management's sub-three-year payback and 13%+ blended ROIC.
测试1——基建是否创造经济价值？是的，计算部分如此。我们最近发布报告中的vintage模型使用：满负荷利用率下每1元资本支出对应1元年收入，45%的增量EBITDA利润率，20%税率，五年直线经济寿命，以及60%/90%/100%的利用率爬坡。每100元资本支出，税后现金流为(100)/25.6/36.4/40.0/40.0/40.0，给出22%的项目IRR、2.9年回收期和10%折现率下36元人民币的NPV。这与管理层的低于三年回收期和13%以上的混合ROIC相一致。
The qualification is use of proceeds. Alibaba described the purpose as full-stack AI capabilities including infrastructure. Model training, application inference and customer acquisition are expensed inside AI Labs and Applications, which lost RMB13.9bn in the quarter, and have no auditable return today. We therefore adopt 60% infrastructure as the base mix and assume no measurable return on the non-infrastructure tranche.
限定条件是资金用途。阿里巴巴将目的描述为包括基础设施在内的全栈AI能力。模型训练、应用推理和客户获取在AI实验室和应用中费用化，该部门季度亏损139亿元人民币，目前没有可审计的回报。因此，我们采用60%基建作为基准组合，并假设非基建部分没有可衡量的回报。

Test 2：EPS——80%以上用于基建时第2年起增厚；60%基准下约中性；闲置现金在所有情景下稀释
Test 2 - Does the transaction increase EPS? It depends on mix. At 80% or more infrastructure, the placement becomes accretive from year two. At our 60% base case, it is 0.5% dilutive in FY28E and roughly neutral thereafter. The same result expressed through ROIC is that year-three accretion requires a mature ROIC of roughly 15% when proceeds are substantially deployed. Cash left idle is dilutive under every scenario, which is why the September-quarter capex print matters.
测试2——交易是否增加EPS？取决于组合。在80%或以上用于基建时，配售从第二年起开始增厚。在我们60%的基准情景下，FY28E稀释0.5%，此后大致中性。通过ROIC表达的相同结果是，当资金大幅部署时，第三年增厚需要约15%的成熟ROIC。闲置现金在所有情景下都是稀释的，这就是为什么9季度资本支出数据很重要。

Test 3：股权不是最便宜的融资来源——债务对EPS好约3ppt，但股权有离岸货币、评级空间、永久资本等合理收益
Test 3 - Was equity the cheapest funding source? No. The debt counterfactual is 3-3.5ppt better on each EPS line because RMB coupons of 2-3% cost far less than a 12% earnings yield. The case for equity rests outside the EPS bridge: offshore currency for procurement, rating and covenant headroom no collateral, and permanent capital that does not need refinancing in the middle of a capex cycle. Those are legitimate benefits, but the choice was not free.
测试3——股权是最便宜的融资来源吗？不是。债务的反事实在每条EPS线上好3-3.5个百分点，因为2-3%的人民币票息成本远低于12%的收益率。股权的理由在EPS桥之外：用于采购的离岸货币、评级和契约空间、无抵押品、以及在资本支出周期中间不需要再融资的永久资本。这些是合理的收益，但选择不是免费的。

Test 4：按合理估值每股内在价值损失约1%，并非市场定价的价值毁灭
Test 4 - Does it create value per existing share? This is the test an earnings-yield comparison does not answer. Issuing below fair value transfers value to new holders, so project NPV must exceed that transfer for existing holders to be better off. Using HK$205 as pre-deal fair value, post-deal value per share is HK$203.1 with full infrastructure NPV, a 0.9% reduction, and HK$202.6 at the 60% mix, a 1.2% reduction. The deal becomes per-share accretive only if pre-deal fair value is below roughly HK$153 at 100% infrastructure or HK$137 at our 60% mix.
测试4——它是否为每股现有股份创造价值？这是收益率比较无法回答的测试。以低于公允价值发行会将价值转移给新股东，因此项目NPV必须超过该转移，现有股东才能受益。使用205港元作为交易前公允价值，交易后每股价值为203.1港元（含全部基建NPV），减少0.9%；在60%组合下为202.6港元，减少1.2%。只有当交易前公允价值低于约153港元（100%基建）或137港元（我们60%组合）时，交易才会每股增厚。

四测试总结：计算创造价值；配售在基准组合下约EPS中性；股权比债务贵约3ppt EPS；现有股东放弃约1%内在价值
Four-test summary: compute creates value; the placement is roughly EPS-neutral at our base mix and accretive if infrastructure share is high; equity costs about 3ppt of EPS versus debt; and existing holders give up about 1% of intrinsic value at our fair value. None of that resembles a US$19bn abnormal pricing, but the accretion claim is conditional, and the next two prints will reveal the condition.
四测试总结：计算创造价值；配售在我们的基准组合下大致EPS中性，如果基建占比高则增厚；股权比债务成本高约3个百分点的EPS；现有股东按我们的合理价值放弃约1%的内在价值。这些都不像190亿美元的异常定价，但增厚主张是有条件的，接下来的两份报告将揭示条件。
Capex trajectory and earnings absorption（资本支出轨迹与盈利吸收）

FY27E资本支出预测不变2000亿，压力情景2700亿；回报不能简单随规模扩大，利用率和定价同时下降时IRR骤降
The duration framing needs updating, but our FY27 capex estimate does not. A US$10.3bn equity raise five days after the print is evidence that management expects capex to stay high for longer than one procurement cycle. We keep FY27E capex at RMB200bn and use RMB270bn as the stress case.
持续时间框架需要更新，但我们的FY27资本支出预测不变。在报告发布五天后进行103亿美元股权融资，证明管理层预计资本支出将在超过一个采购周期内保持高位。我们维持FY27E资本支出为2000亿元人民币，并使用2700亿元人民币作为压力情景。
Returns cannot simply be scaled with capex. A larger program at constant unit economics creates more value; the risk is that unit returns fall as supply expands. Flexing mature utilization and pricing together, the project still clears a 10% cost of capital across most of the grid. But 80% utilization with 10% lower pricing earns only about 11%, and 70% utilization with 20% lower pricing earns about 3%. This is how a bigger program becomes a worse one, and why RMB270bn is a risk case rather than upside.
回报不能简单随资本支出规模扩大。在恒定单位经济性下更大的计划创造更多价值；风险在于单位回报随供给扩张而下降。同时调整成熟利用率和定价，项目在大部分网格下仍能清除10%的资本成本。但80%利用率加10%降价仅获得约11%，70%利用率加20%降价仅获得约3%。这就是更大的计划如何变成更差的计划，以及为什么2700亿元人民币是风险情景而非上行情景。

Trading the funding cycle（融资周期交易）

日期事件：8月26日配售结算消除技术压力；百度同日EGM表决南向通；阿里巴巴9季报是主要测试
Dated events. Placement settlement on 26 August removes the technical overhang, while Baidu's EGM the same day gates the Southbound-flow story. Alibaba's September-quarter print is the main test: external cloud growth above 50%, AI Labs losses down sequentially, and capex consistent with prefunding. Hunyuan 4 is Tencent's evidence point; the Kunlunxin listing is Baidu's key funding event because it changes the ability to scale.
日期事件。8月26日配售结算消除了技术压力，而百度同日的股东特别大会表决了南向通故事。阿里巴巴的9季度报告是主要测试：外部云增长超过50%、AI实验室亏损环比下降、以及资本支出与预融资一致。混元4是腾讯的证据点；昆仑万维上市是百度的关键融资事件，因为它改变了扩展能力。
Precedents. BYD and Xiaomi both placed equity at 6-8% discounts in March 2025; both stocks opened lower and were later absorbed into rallies already under way. We treat those deals as context rather than valuation anchors for Alibaba.
先例。比亚迪和小米都在2025年3月以6-8%的折让进行了股权配售；两只股票均低开，随后被已在进行的上涨吸收。我们将这些交易视为背景，而非阿里巴巴的估值锚。
Relative positioning. Tencent's premium multiple buys a fully internally funded program plus a RMB875bn portfolio. Alibaba has removed a funding overhang, while we think the market has over-priced the negative signal embedded in the raise. With the stock trading at 12x FY27E and 8x FY28E P/E, we would add into the September-quarter print. Baidu's parent dilution risk remains low because its equity route is at the subsidiary level.
相对定位。腾讯的溢价倍数购买了完全内部融资的计划加上8750亿元人民币的投资组合。阿里巴巴消除了融资压力，而我们认为市场对融资中嵌入的负面信号定价过高。该股交易于12倍FY27E和8倍FY28E市盈率，我们会在9季度报告前加仓。百度的母公司稀释风险仍然很低，因为其股权路径在子公司层面。

什么会改变我们的观点：七个可观察风险
What would change our view
• Alibaba raises its three-year capex commitment materially above RMB380bn without lifting external cloud growth guidance above 50%.
• 阿里巴巴将其三年资本支出承诺大幅提高至3800亿元人民币以上，而未将外部云增长指引提升至50%以上。
• Proceeds remain substantially undeployed for more than 12 months, visible in treasury balances rather than PP&E and prepayments.
• 资金在超过12个月内基本未部署，可见于国库余额而非固定资产和预付款。
• AI Labs and Applications losses fail to decline sequentially in the September quarter, implying a lower infrastructure share of spending.
• AI实验室和应用亏损在9季度未能环比下降，意味着支出中基建占比更低。
• External cloud pricing deflates as supply eases; Figure 8 shows project IRR near 11% at 80% utilization and 10% lower pricing.
• 外部云定价随供给宽松而下降；图8显示在80%利用率和10%降价下项目IRR接近11%。
• A second parent-level Alibaba equity raise within 12 months.
• 阿里巴巴在12个月内进行第二次母公司层面股权融资。
• For Tencent, a quarter in which capex + dividend + buyback exceeds operating cash flow without a matching disposal.
• 对腾讯而言，出现一个季度资本支出+股息+回购超过经营现金流而无匹配处置的情况。
• For Baidu, a Kunlunxin listing delayed beyond 2026 or raising materially less than needed for the stated capacity plan.
• 对百度而言，昆仑万维上市延迟至2026年以后，或融资额远低于所述产能计划所需。
