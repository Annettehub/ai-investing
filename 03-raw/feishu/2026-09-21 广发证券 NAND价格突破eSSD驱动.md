eSSD/AI需求是关键驱动：企业需求显著超过消费端

eSSD/AI demand the key driver amid suppliers' increased confidence: According to TrendForce, a sharp structural split is emerging in NAND, with enterprise demand materially outpacing consumer segments. eSSD pull-ins remain strong, particularly from U.S. CSPs scaling AI infrastructure, with both TLC and increasingly QLC SSDs seeing accelerated uptake. Confidence in this demand is underpinned by AI workloads shifting from training toward agentic-AI, vector databases, and KV-cache offloading, where eSSD offers a superior cost-per-bit vs. DRAM and better power efficiency vs. HDD.
eSSD/AI需求是供应商信心增强中的关键驱动：据TrendForce，NAND正在出现明显的结构性分化，企业需求显著超过消费端。eSSD提前拉货依然强劲，特别是来自扩大AI基础设施的美国CSP，TLC和越来越多的QLC SSD都在加速采用。对这一需求的信心得到以下事实支撑：AI工作负载从训练转向agentic-AI、向量数据库和KV-cache卸载，在这些场景中eSSD在cost-per-bit上优于DRAM，在能效上优于HDD。



AI重塑存储层级：新增SCM层，QLC NAND成为AI数据湖批量层

AI to reshape the storage hierarchy leading to content gains of NAND: AI workloads are breaking the traditional memory–storage hierarchy that relied on a clean separation between DRAM → NAND (SLC/MLC/TLC/QLC) → HDD. However, AI introduces continuous, high-throughput, low-latency access to massive datasets. This gap is being filled by Storage Class Memory (SCM), a new tier "near-memory storage," delivering better latency and DRAM-like read performance. Below SCM, QLC NAND becomes the bulk layer for AI data lakes, providing extreme density and cost efficiency for petabyte-scale training corpora. The result is a more granular, AI-specific hierarchy: DRAM → SCM → TLC/QLC NAND → HDD.
AI重塑存储层级，推动NAND内容增益：AI工作负载正在打破传统的内存-存储层级，该层级依赖DRAM→NAND（SLC/MLC/TLC/QLC）→HDD之间的清晰分离。然而，AI引入了对海量数据集的持续、高吞吐量、低延迟访问。这一空白正由存储级内存（SCM）填补，这是一个"近内存存储"新层，提供更好的延迟和类似DRAM的读取性能。在SCM之下，QLC NAND成为AI数据湖的批量层，为PB级训练语料库提供极高密度和成本效率。结果是一个更细粒度的、AI特有的层级：DRAM→SCM→TLC/QLC NAND→HDD。
