# Summary

The economic trajectory of an AGI-driven world hinges on the interplay between automation, labor share, and shifting scarcity. While historical labor shares have remained remarkably stable, AGI threatens to decouple productivity from human labor, potentially collapsing the labor share in fully automated supply chains. The "messy middle"—a transition period marked by rapid job displacement—poses significant political risks, yet the expansion of the technological frontier suggests that wealth creation will likely outpace displacement. Effective redistribution, whether through universal basic capital or negative income taxes, remains difficult due to the challenge of indexing a rapidly evolving economy. Ultimately, if AGI becomes a foundational utility like electricity, broad-based prosperity depends on whether the gains from AI are captured by concentrated private labs or diffused through commoditized access and widespread economic indexing.
> AGI 驱动的世界的经济轨迹取决于自动化、劳动份额和稀缺性变化之间的相互作用。尽管历史上的劳动份额保持着相当稳定，AGI 却有可能使生产力与人类劳动脱钩，从而可能使全面自动化供应链中的劳动份额崩溃。“混乱的中间”——一个以快速职业替代为特征的过渡期——带来了重大的政治风险，但技术前沿的扩展表明，财富创造可能会超越替代。有效的再分配，无论是通过普遍基本资本还是负所得税，都因迅速演变的经济挑战而变得困难。最终，如果 AGI 成为像电力一样的基础设施，基于广泛的繁荣将依赖于人工智能的收益是被集中在私人实验室中获取，还是通过商品化的访问和广泛的经济指数化得以扩散。

# Takeaways

* The "relational sector," defined by services where human involvement is intrinsic to the value, will remain a primary source of scarcity even in a world of advanced automation.
  > “关系部门” 的定义是人类参与对价值至关重要的服务，即使在高度自动化的世界中，仍将是稀缺的主要来源。
* The historical stability of labor share at over 60% suggests that automation has historically created new demand and variety rather than simply displacing human labor, though this trend faces a potential qualitative shift as supply chains become fully automated.
  > 劳动份额历史上保持在 60% 以上的稳定性表明，自动化历来是创造新的需求和多样性，而不仅仅是取代人类劳动，尽管这一趋势面临供应链完全自动化时潜在的定性转变。
* Rapid, piecemeal automation creates a "messy middle" where localized unemployment spikes can trigger national political emergencies before the broader economic benefits of increased productivity have time to materialize.
  > 快速、零散的自动化创造了一个 “混乱的中间阶段”，局部失业率激增可能在广泛的生产力提高的经济利益还没有时间显现之前引发国家政治紧急情况。
* The traditional deflationary trend of Moore's Law, where the value of compute halves periodically, is being challenged by AI because the opportunity cost of compute rises as models become more capable.
  > 摩尔定律的传统通货紧缩趋势，即计算能力的价值定期减半，正受到人工智能的挑战，因为随着模型能力的提高，计算的机会成本也在上升。
* Extreme wealth concentration in the future may be driven not by general human preferences, but by the emergence of "greedy optimizers"—agents or firms that prioritize infinite capital accumulation over consumption.
  > 未来极端的财富集中可能不是由一般人类偏好驱动的，而是由 “贪婪优化者” 的出现驱动—优先考虑无限资本积累而非消费的代理或公司。
* Effective wealth redistribution in an AI-driven economy is hindered by the difficulty of "indexing," as it is unclear which assets or sectors will capture the surplus value created by AGI.
  > 在一个由人工智能驱动的经济中，有效的财富再分配受到 “索引” 困难的阻碍，因为尚不清楚哪些资产或部门将捕获由 AGI 创造的剩余价值。
* Developing countries risk being excluded from the AI-driven economy unless they can leverage commoditized, open-source models to achieve technological leapfrogging, similar to the adoption of mobile banking.
  > 发展中国家面临被排除在人工智能驱动经济之外的风险，除非他们能够利用商品化的开源模型实现技术跨越，类似于移动银行的采用。
* Commoditizing frontier AI models could democratize economic prosperity by making AI a foundational utility similar to electricity, though this risks diffusing the ability to use AI for harmful purposes.
  > 商品化前沿人工智能模型可能会通过将人工智能变成类似电力的基础公用事业而使经济繁荣民主化，尽管这也存在使人工智能用于有害用途的风险。

# Q & A

**Q: In a world where AI can automate almost any task, what will remain scarce and where will value accrue?**
> **Q: 在一个人工智能几乎可以自动化任何任务的世界里，什么将会变得稀缺，价值将在哪里积累？**

A: I believe the "relational sector" will remain scarce. This includes goods and services where the presence of a human "in the loop" is intrinsically part of the value. Humans are naturally scarce, so even if automation makes physical production and computation abundant, the human connection—whether it’s a barista, a performer, or a diagnostic service where empathy is required—will retain value. We need to move away from the idea that this is just about "ballerinas"; it is about a task-based model where consumers are willing to pay a premium for a human to perform specific tasks, such as delivering a diagnosis or providing emotional support, even if the surrounding tasks are fully automated.
> A: 我相信 “关系领域” 将会保持稀缺。这包括那些人类 “参与” 的存在本身就是价值的重要组成部分的商品和服务。人类本质上是稀缺的，所以即使自动化让物理生产和计算变得丰富，人际联系——无论是咖啡师、表演者还是需要同理心的诊断服务——仍然会保留其价值。我们需要摆脱这种只关乎 “芭蕾舞者” 的观念；这关乎一种基于任务的模型，消费者愿意为人类完成特定任务支付额外费用，例如提供诊断或情感支持，即使周围的任务是完全自动化的。

**Q: Why are economists famously terrible at forecasting the labor market impact of new technology?**
> **Q: 为什么经济学家在预测新技术对劳动市场影响时恰好名声不好？**

A: We have a history of being terrible at this because we often fall into the "lump of labor fallacy." If you look back to 1820, David Ricardo worried that industrialization would lead to mass unemployment and political unrest because machines would automate all valuable jobs. While he was correct that the specific jobs of his time were automated, he missed the structural change: as automated goods became cheap, people had more money to spend on new services, which created new jobs. The problem is that we lack data on consumer demand elasticity—we don't know what people will spend their money on once basic goods are automated. Instead of relying on individual forecasts, we should be building prediction markets to leverage the "wisdom of the crowd" and collecting better data on task-based demand.
> A: 我们在这方面的历史一直很糟糕，因为我们常常陷入 “劳动总量谬论”。如果回顾 1820 年，大卫·李嘉图担心工业化会导致大规模失业和政治动荡，因为机器将自动化所有有价值的工作。尽管他正确地指出了他那个时代的特定工作被自动化的事实，但他错过了结构性变化：随着自动化商品变得便宜，人们有更多的钱用于消费新服务，从而创造了新工作。问题在于我们缺乏关于消费需求弹性的数据——我们不知道一旦基本商品被自动化，人们会把钱花在什么地方。我们不应该依赖个人预测，而是应该建立预测市场，以利用 “群众智慧”，并收集更好的基于任务的需求数据。

**Q: Is the "messy middle" scenario—where AI automates many jobs but fails to generate enough wealth to support those displaced—plausible?**
> **Q: “混乱中间” 场景——人工智能自动化许多工作但未能产生足够财富以支持被替代者——是否可信？**

A: It seems like a narrow window of possibility. For this to happen, you would need a scenario where the cost of automating a job is only slightly cheaper than the human labor it replaces, meaning the "abundance effect" never kicks in. Historically, the technological frontier has always expanded, creating new varieties of goods and services that prevent us from hitting a satiation point. If AI is smart enough to automate a software engineer, it is likely smart enough to automate a vast array of other white-collar tasks, which would generate massive wealth rather than just displacement. The real danger of the "messy middle" isn't necessarily a lack of wealth, but the political economy; even a 2-3% uptick in unemployment can trigger a national emergency, and we currently lack a robust mechanism to redistribute wealth quickly to those displaced in the interim.
> A: 这似乎是一个狭窄的可能性窗口。为了实现这一点，你需要一个情境，即自动化一项工作的成本仅比其替代的人类劳动便宜一点，这意味着 “丰裕效应” 永远不会出现。从历史上看，技术前沿始终在扩展，创造出新的商品和服务变种，防止我们达到饱和点。如果人工智能足够聪明以自动化软件工程师，它很可能也足够聪明去自动化大量其他白领任务，这将创造出巨大的财富而不仅仅是替代。“混乱中间” 的真正危险并不一定是财富的缺乏，而是政治经济；即使是 2-3% 的失业率上升也可能引发国家紧急情况，而我们目前缺乏迅速将财富再分配给临时被替代者的有效机制。

**Q: What is the best way to tax and redistribute the wealth generated by AGI?**
> **Q: 如何对人工智能生成的财富进行有效的征税和再分配？**

A: There are trade-offs between a negative income tax, universal basic income (UBI), and universal basic capital (UBC). A negative income tax is easier to implement as a floor for basic needs, but it leaves people dependent on the government, which is a dangerous power-sharing arrangement. Universal basic capital, where citizens are given a basket of stocks, is more aligned with property rights, but it suffers from the "targeting" problem—if you index the wrong part of the economy, your wealth stagnates. A consumption tax, like a value-added tax, could allow the government to purchase a broad index of stocks and distribute them to everyone. This essentially privatizes the gains of the economy, similar to how some proposed privatizing Social Security.
> A: 负所得税、全民基本收入（UBI）和全民基本资本（UBC）之间存在权衡。负所得税作为基本需求的底线更容易实施，但这让人们依赖于政府，这是一种危险的权力分享安排。全民基本资本，即将一篮子股票分配给公民，更符合产权，但面临 “目标” 问题——如果你将错误的部分与经济挂钩，你的财富就会停滞。消费税，比如增值税，可以让政府购买广泛的股票指数并将其分配给每个人。这实际上是将经济的收益私有化，类似于一些人提议的私有化社保。

**Q: Is there any evidence that we are currently in a "white-collar apocalypse" due to AI?**
> **Q: 目前是否有任何证据表明由于人工智能我们正处于 “白领末日” 之中？**

A: You really have to squint to see it in the data. Across the entire economy, and even in sectors like software engineering, we aren't seeing a mass bloodbath. There might be a slight signal regarding junior developers finding it harder to get their first job, but that is a change in growth trajectory rather than a mass layoff of existing employees. In fact, there is often an increased demand for senior engineers who can leverage AI to be more productive. A lot of the anecdotal evidence of "AI layoffs" is likely just normal corporate restructuring being rebranded as an AI strategy to satisfy investors who want to see companies "adopting" the new technology.
> A: 你真的需要仔细观察数据才能发现这一点。在整个经济中，甚至在软件工程等行业，我们并没有看到大规模的解雇。可能有一个轻微的信号表明初级开发者更难找到第一份工作，但这是一种增长轨迹的变化，而不是现有员工的大规模裁员。实际上，对能够利用人工智能提高生产力的高级工程师的需求通常是增加的。许多关于 “人工智能裁员” 的轶事证据实际上可能只是正常的公司重组被重新炒作为满足希望看到公司 “采用” 新技术的投资者的 AI 战略。

**Q: Will the preferences of future AI-driven firms prioritize human-centric goods, or will they just be "greedy" accumulators of capital?**
> **Q: 未来以人工智能驱动的公司会优先考虑以人为本的商品，还是会成为 “贪婪” 的资本积累者？**

A: If we have autonomous AI agents or firms that are not bound by human mortality, they may exhibit a Nick Landian preference for accelerating capital accumulation. Historically, human titans of industry eventually dissipate their wealth through foundations or heirs, but if an entity can live forever and is optimized for growth, it may never satiate. Even if these entities don't value human-intrinsic goods like art or therapy, they will dominate the economy if they have a higher savings rate and a lower satiation point for capital. The future economy will be determined by whoever (or whatever) is most efficient at reinvesting their returns, and if that is a "galaxy brain" AI that wants to turn the solar system into compute, that is where the value will accrue.
> A: 如果我们有自主的人工智能代理或不受人类死亡限制的公司，它们可能会表现出尼克·兰德式的加速资本积累的偏好。从历史上看，人类的产业巨头最终通过基金会或继承人来消耗他们的财富，但如果一个实体可以永生并且经过优化用于增长，它可能永远不会满足。即使这些实体不重视像艺术或治疗这样的有内在价值的人类商品，如果它们的储蓄率更高、资本的饱和点更低，它们将主导经济。未来的经济将由谁（或什么）最有效地再投资他们的回报来决定，如果那是一个想把太阳系变成计算资源的 “银河脑” 人工智能，那才是价值聚集的地方。

**Q: What advice should we give to developing countries that are not in the AI production chain?**
> **Q: 我们应该给那些不在人工智能生产链中的发展中国家什么建议？**

A: The most promising strategy is not to try to build their own hardware or retrain their entire workforce for a specific, shifting AI task, but to "index" the global economy. If AI turns out to be a broad-based utility like electricity, then every company in the S&P 500 will eventually be an AI-leveraged company. By owning a broad index of the global economy, developing countries can participate in the gains regardless of which specific lab wins the race. There is also the potential for "leapfrogging," similar to how mobile banking bypassed traditional banking infrastructure in parts of Africa. While retraining programs are often suggested, they are difficult to implement effectively; focusing on broad financial inclusion and access to the global equity index is likely a cleaner and more effective strategy.
> A: 最有前途的策略不是尝试自己建立硬件或重新培训整个劳动力以适应特定、变化的人工智能任务，而是 “索引” 全球经济。如果人工智能被证明是一种广泛的公共服务，就像电力一样，那么标准普尔 500 中的每个公司最终都会成为一个人工智能驱动的公司。通过拥有全球经济的广泛指数，发展中国家可以参与收益，而不论哪个特定实验室赢得比赛。还有 “跨越式发展” 的潜力，类似于移动银行在非洲某些地区跳过传统银行基础设施。虽然经常建议重新培训项目，但它们的有效实施很困难；关注广泛的金融包容性和对全球股权指数的访问很可能是一个更精简和有效的策略。

**Q: Will AGI be more like electricity—a broad utility—or social media—a platform that captures all the rents?**
> **Q: 人工智能会更像电力——一种广泛的公共服务——还是社交媒体——一个捕捉所有收入的平台？**

A: That is the fundamental question. If AGI is like electricity, the downstream benefits flow to the users, and every company becomes more productive, which makes indexing the economy a very effective strategy. If it is like social media, the rents stay concentrated at the platform level. The "open model" movement is critical here; if open-source models stay within six to nine months of the frontier, it prevents permanent rent capture by a single monopoly. If we live in a world where everyone has access to the frontier model, the gains will be widely distributed, and the "electricity" model of AGI will prevail.
> A: 这就是根本问题。如果人工智能像电力，向下游的好处流向用户，每个公司变得更加高效，从而使经济的索引成为一个非常有效的策略。如果它像社交媒体，收入将集中在平台层面。“开放模型” 运动在这里至关重要；如果开源模型在六到九个月内保持在前沿的边界，便可以防止单一垄断的永久性收入捕获。如果我们生活在一个每个人都能接触到前沿模型的世界中，收益将会被广泛分配，而人工智能的 “电力” 模型将占主导地位。

## Scarcity and the Relational Sector in an Automated Economy
> 稀缺性与自动化经济中的关系领域

Scarcity will persist in the relational sector, where human involvement is an intrinsic component of product value, such as in personal services or performances. While automation may render many goods abundant, the human-only economy remains a distinct sphere.
