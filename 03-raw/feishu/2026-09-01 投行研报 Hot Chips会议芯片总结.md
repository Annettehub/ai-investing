一、GOOGLE (TPU 8) Session

Google TPU 8：架构推理优先，而非单纯FLOPS
Our key takeaway from Google's presentation was not the TPU 8 specifications themselves (which were largely discussed earlier this year) but rather the architectural reasoning behind Google's growing separation of training and inference silicon.
Google演讲的核心要点不是TPU 8规格本身，而是Google日益分离训练和推理芯片背后的架构推理。
Google's team emphasized that modern reasoning, MOE, and agentic inference workloads are becoming increasingly memory-bound and compute-light, which drove the development of the dedicated TPU SI training accelerator (a 2-die SRAM). As a result, TPU 8 incorporates 384MB of on-chip SRAM, 288GB of HBM3E, and 192TB/s of inter-chip bandwidth, prioritizing memory capacity, memory bandwidth, and low-latency communication over raw compute density.
Google团队强调，现代推理、MOE和agentic推理工作负载正变得越来越内存受限而计算轻量，这推动了专用TPU SI训练加速器的开发。TPU 8集成384MB片上SRAM、288GB HBM3E和192TB/s芯片间带宽，优先内存容量/带宽/低延迟通信。
By contrast, the TPU SI (made by Broadcom) was designed for large-scale pre-training and model development, sharing of 9,000 chips per memory, 121 Exaflops of FP4 compute. Highlighting Google's view that training and inference are now diverging enough to justify separate architectures.
相比之下，TPU SI（Broadcom制造）专为大规模预训练和模型开发设计，每个内存共享9,000颗芯片、121 Exaflops FP4计算。凸显Google观点：训练和推理差异足够大，值得独立架构。
Overall, the presentation reinforced Google's belief that the next bottleneck in AI infrastructure is increasingly memory movement and communication rather than pure FLOPS, particularly as agentic AI introduces longer context windows, more reasoning steps, and heavier inter-chip communication.
总体而言，演讲强化了Google信念：AI基础设施下一个瓶颈正日益成为内存移动和通信，而非单纯FLOPS，特别是agentic AI引入更长上下文、更多推理步骤和更重芯片间通信。
二、LPX (Groq/NVIDIA) Session

LPX 3：定位为complementary，而非Vera Rubin替代品
Our key takeaway from the LPX presentation was not the rack-scale hardware itself but rather the growing focus on heterogeneous inference and workload dispatch for agents.
LPX演讲核心要点不是rack-scale硬件本身，而是日益关注异构推理和agent工作负载调度。
The NVDIA team argued that increasingly complex reasoning workloads are creating a bifurcation between throughput-optimized GPUs and latency-optimized inference accelerators, positioning LPX 3 as a complementary architectural piece for the post-training GPU era.
NVIDIA团队认为，日益复杂的推理工作负载正造成吞吐量优化GPU和延迟优化推理加速器之间的分化，将LPX 3定位为后训练GPU时代的互补架构组件。
The most incremental detail of the presentation was new benchmarking and architectural detail around these disaggregated systems, where NVIDIA showed that combining LPX with Rubin can improve user interactivity and throughput efficiency by integrating 256 fully SRAM-based design with no external DRAM, LPGX leverages a fully SRAM-based design of 315 PetaFLOPS FP8 compute, 40nbps of per-chip memory bandwidth, and 35ns chip-to-chip latency, which enables ultra-low-latency inference for decode-heavy workloads.
演讲最新增细节是关于这些分解系统的新基准测试和架构细节，NVIDIA展示LPX与Rubin结合可通过256全SRAM设计（无外部DRAM）提高用户交互性和吞吐量效率，LPGX采用全SRAM设计，315 PetaFLOPS FP8计算、40nbps每芯片内存带宽、35ns芯片间延迟，为解码密集工作负载实现超低延迟推理。
Overall, we think LPX solution would be a key part of NVIDIA's specialized accelerators for the agentic AI stack as the cost per token declines with pagination, while the GPU stack remains optimized for throughput-intensive with disaggregation of agentic AI workload as the most promising near-term opportunity.
总体而言，我们认为LPX解决方案将成为NVIDIA面向agentic AI栈专用加速器的关键部分，随着分页降低每token成本，GPU栈仍针对吞吐量密集型优化，agentic AI工作负载分解是最有前景的近期机会。
三、d-Matrix (Raptor 3D-DRAM)

d-Matrix Raptor：3D-DRAM加速器，消除memory refresh overhead
d-Matrix introduced its Raptor 3D-DRAM accelerator in its Hot Chips presentation on Sunday. Notably, d-Matrix brought a meta engine on stage alongside its CTO, underscoring hyperfocused interest in alternative inference architectures for frontier-scale models.
d-Matrix在周日Hot Chips演讲中介绍Raptor 3D-DRAM加速器。值得注意的是，d-Matrix与CTO一同带来meta引擎，凸显对前沿规模模型替代推理架构的高度关注。
We found the detailed architectural discussion around 3D-DRAM insightful as d-Matrix argued that vertically integrated compute-on-memory designs can deliver efficiency gains beyond conventional HBM-based systems.
我们发现关于3D-DRAM的详细架构讨论很有洞察力，d-Matrix认为垂直集成内存计算设计可带来超越传统基于HBM系统的效率提升。
Raptor combines a TSMC 4nm logic die with a stacked 3D-DRAM die using face-to-face integration, enabling 32GB per card, ~100TB/s of memory bandwidth, and support for 1M-token context forward models within a 72-card rack deployment.
Raptor将台积电4nm逻辑die与堆叠3D-DRAM die采用面对面集成，实现每卡32GB、约100TB/s内存带宽，在72卡机架部署中支持1M-token上下文前向模型。
The company also highlighted several novel mechanisms, including streaming block to eliminate memory refresh overheads, DDR4 for 20% I/O power savings, and thermal-aware refresh mechanisms that minimize bandwidth loss at low operating temperatures.
该公司还强调几种新颖机制，包括流式块消除内存刷新开销、DDR4实现20% I/O功耗节省、热感知刷新机制在低工作温度下最小化带宽损失。
Overall, d-Matrix is continuing to focus on work that we think could become increasingly relevant for inference- and KV-cache-heavy differentiated AI workloads-particularly as memory bandwidth emerges as a primary bottleneck in long-context reasoning.
总体而言，d-Matrix继续专注于我们认为对推理和KV-cache密集型差异化AI工作负载可能日益相关的工作——特别是内存带宽成为长上下文推理主要瓶颈时。
四、Cerebras (WSE-4) Session

Cerebras WSE-4：晶圆级架构，CS-4系统，2028年CS-6预计3D DRAM
Our key takeaway from Cerebras' session was largely consistent with what the company presented at Supernova event on Aug 18th, with CBRS again emphasizing the advantages of wafer-scale architecture and the newly introduced CS-4 system.
Cerebras会议核心要点与该公司8月18日Supernova活动展示基本一致，CBRS再次强调晶圆级架构优势和新推出的CS-4系统。
CS-4 is designed to address the growing inference bottleneck up to 30x faster inference versus GPUs, 25x higher memory, with Cerebras claiming throughput through 3x and sub-scale neural improvements in memory, fabric, and I/O through its wafer-scale architecture.
CS-4旨在解决日益增长的推理瓶颈，推理速度比GPU快30倍，内存高25倍，Cerebras声称通过晶圆级架构在内存、fabric和I/O方面实现3倍吞吐量和亚尺度神经改进。
The company's core thesis remains that scaling AI is increasingly constrained by memory bandwidth, latency, and interconnect overhead rather than pure compute, which reduces dependency on complex multi-GPU fabrics, while increasing throughput.
该公司核心论点仍然是，扩展AI正日益受到内存带宽、延迟和互连开销限制，而非纯计算，这减少了对复杂多GPU fabric的依赖，同时提高吞吐量。
The most incremental detail in our view was the early look at CS-6, which introduces 3D-stacked DRAM on top of the Cerebras wafer-scale architecture, combining wafer-scale SRAM with vertically integrated DRAM to enable larger models in a substrate smaller footprint while preserving the latency advantages of its architecture.
我们认为最新增细节是对CS-6的早期展望，它在Cerebras晶圆级架构之上引入3D堆叠DRAM，将晶圆级SRAM与垂直集成DRAM结合，在更小基板占用空间内支持更大模型，同时保留其架构延迟优势。
Cerebras has started its DRAM enabling advantages of its architecture in 2024 and the company has not provided an official timeline. We understand the annual cadence CS-6 will likely be available in 2028.
Cerebras于2024年开始其DRAM使能优势，该公司尚未提供官方时间表。我们了解年度节奏CS-6可能在2028年可用。
We think 3D DRAM roadmap represents one of the more important developments, allowing memory bandwidth above SRAM while still benefiting from high memory bandwidth.
我们认为3D DRAM路线图代表了更重要的发展之一，允许内存带宽超越SRAM，同时仍受益于高内存带宽。
五、SambaNova Accelerator Session

SambaNova SN50 RDU：5nm 640 RDU，全风冷，企业级推理定位
SambaNova presented its new SN50 RDU (reconfigurable dataflow unit) addressing the next-generation agentic AI workloads that it thinks are increasingly constrained by memory-bandwidth utilization rather than peak compute.
SambaNova展示新SN50 RDU（可重构数据流单元），针对下一代agentic AI工作负载，该公司认为这些工作负载正日益受到内存带宽利用率而非峰值计算限制。
The new SN50 is designed to address large-scale, frontier-model inference through a unique dataflow architecture that emphasizes operator fusion, data locality, and minimizing synchronization overheads, while supporting large-scale deployments through Ethernet-based scale-up and scale-out networks.
新SN50旨在通过独特数据流架构解决大规模前沿模型推理，强调算子融合、数据局部性和最小化同步开销，同时通过基于以太网的scale-up和scale-out网络支持大规模部署。
Importantly, SambaNova highlighted that its rack-scale solution is fully air-cooled, which we view as a meaningful differentiator for enterprise on-prem deployments where retrofitting datacenter infrastructure is often not configured for liquid cooling.
重要的是，SambaNova强调其机架级解决方案完全风冷，我们认为这对企业本地部署是有意义的差异化因素，因为改造数据中心基础设施通常不配置液冷。
At the chip level, the 5nm CoWoS-based SN50 RDU delivers 3,200 FP8 TFLOPS, integrates 432MB of on-chip SRAM, 64GB of HBM with 1.84TB/s of bandwidth, and adds a CXL-attached DDR memory tier (100GB/s) for prompt caching, creating a three-tier memory hierarchy (SRAM, HBM, DDR) designed to maximize model-and-fabric utilization for increasingly bandwidth-bound agentic and frontier-model inference workloads.
芯片层面，基于5nm CoWoS的SN50 RDU提供3,200 FP8 TFLOPS，集成432MB片上SRAM、64GB HBM（1.84TB/s带宽），增加CXL连接DDR内存层（100GB/s）用于prompt缓存，创建三层内存层次结构（SRAM、HBM、DDR），为带宽受限的agentic和前沿模型推理工作负载最大化模型和fabric利用率。
the chip also incorporates 2TB/s of Ethernet scale-up bandwidth and supports scaling to 256+ accelerators within a scale-up domain.
该芯片还集成2TB/s以太网scale-up带宽，支持在scale-up域内扩展到256+加速器。
Overall, we believe SambaNova is positioning itself as an attractive alternative for inference-heavy deployments where ease of deployment, power efficiency, and compatibility with existing datacenter infrastructure can be as important as raw accelerator performance.
总体而言，我们认为SambaNova正将自己定位为推理密集型部署的有吸引力替代方案，在这些部署中，部署便捷性、能效和与现有数据中心基础设施兼容性可能与原始加速器性能同样重要。
六、CPU Session (NVDA + AMD + INTC)

CPU路线图：NVDA Vera CPU、ARM AGI、AMD EPYC、Intel Diamond Rapids
Each company (NVDA, AMD, INTC) highlighted its CPU roadmap, as Agentic AI drives a step-function increase in CPUs to perform workload orchestration.
每家公司（NVDA、AMD、INTC）都强调其CPU路线图，因为Agentic AI推动CPU执行工作负载编排的阶跃函数增长。
Regarding ARM, the company highlighted its AGI CPU - with up to 136 cores, 6GB/s memory bandwidth per core, and overall 2TB of DDR5-8000 per chip.
关于ARM，该公司强调其AGI CPU——高达136核、每核6GB/s内存带宽、每芯片整体2TB DDR5-8000。
We believe ARM's core competency lies in latency and efficiency, which aligns well with hyperscaler needs - however, current limitations in core count/throughput will limit the use of ARM's internal Gen1 AGI for standalone AI racks because on a single machine, workloads need to other hand across as many cores for this single machine as possible.
我们认为ARM核心竞争力在于延迟和效率，这与超大规模云厂商需求非常契合——然而，当前核数/吞吐量限制将限制ARM内部Gen1 AGI用于独立AI机架，因为在单台机器上，工作负载需要尽可能多地跨这台机器核心分配。
INTC's Diamond Rapids consists up to 256 cores with 1.6TB/s memory support and 1.28GB last level cache.
INTC的Diamond Rapids包含高达256核、1.6TB/s内存支持和1.28GB末级缓存。
We think INTC retains optionally with x86, but mid-term upside hinges on improving its high-core-count/performance roadmap.
我们认为INTC在x86方面仍有选择权，但中期上行取决于改进其高核数/性能路线图。
Finally, NVDA's Vera CPU is optimized for agent performance per core - with spatial multithreading, LPDDR5X memory subsystem, and monolithic compute all tailored for agentic AI overall, NVDA highlighted greater power efficiency per watt for the Vera CPU vs traditional CPU.
最后，NVDA的Vera CPU针对每核agent性能优化——具有空间多线程、LPDDR5X内存子系统和单片计算，全部针对agentic AI定制，总体而言，NVDA强调Vera CPU与传统CPU相比每瓦能效更高。
七、AMD FPGA / Memory / Networking

AMD Versal Premium Gen2 + 三星PIM + Broadcom/Nvidia网络
AMD used Hot Chips to broaden the Versal roadmap across physical AI, data center, and RF/edge workloads. Versal Premium Gen2 adds on package LPDDR5X, PCIe Gen7/CXL 4.0, higher DSP density and enhanced security, targeting low-latency physical AI and infrastructure acceleration.
AMD利用Hot Chips扩展跨物理AI、数据中心和RF/边缘工作负载的Versal路线图。Versal Premium Gen2增加封装内LPDDR5X、PCIe Gen7/CXL 4.0、更高DSP密度和增强安全性，针对低延迟物理AI和基础设施加速。
Further, AMD highlighted its next-gen Versal RF adaptive SoCs, integrating RF converters, hardened DSP AI engines and programmable logic on one chip; versus prior-gen RFSoCs, AMD claims up to 19x DSP compute, 6.4x higher sample rates (32 GSPS ADC) and 18GHz bandwidth, targeting defense, communications, test/measurement and emerging quantum-control workloads.
此外，AMD强调下一代Versal RF自适应SoC，单芯片集成RF转换器、硬化DSP AI引擎和可编程逻辑；与上一代RFSoC相比，AMD声称高达19倍DSP计算、6.4倍更高采样率（32 GSPS ADC）和18GHz带宽，针对国防、通信、测试/测量和新兴量子控制工作负载。
Samsung's productized LPDDR5X-PIM emulates MAC compute inside DRAM banks, delivering 614GB/s PIN bandwidth (8x conventional LPDDR5X) and ~3x higher Llama-3.1 8B token throughput, positioning it as a lower-cost/lower-power alternative to HBM. XENA's CXL 3.0 MX1 RISC-V cores integrate 2TB DDR5 + SSD-based memory + 3,072 near-memory RISC-V cores, with Samsung demonstrating rack-scale CXL pooling and 3.5x higher LLM decode throughput at 10K context.
三星产品化LPDDR5X-PIM在DRAM bank内模拟MAC计算，提供614GB/s PIN带宽（8倍传统LPDDR5X）和约3倍Llama-3.1 8B token吞吐量，定位为HBM的低成本/低功耗替代方案。XENA的CXL 3.0 MX1 RISC-V核集成2TB DDR5 + SSD内存 + 3,072 near-memory RISC-V核，三星展示机架级CXL池化和10K上下文下3.5倍更高LLM解码吞吐量。
Networking: Nvidia's Spectrum-X multiplane architecture fans each 1.6T NIC across eight switches, claiming 64x higher scale (8K -> 512K Rubin GPUs), 1.7x fewer switches and better fault resiliency, while BlueField-4 becomes a standard Vera Rubin infrastructure processor with 800G Ethernet / 7Tbps aggregate platform bandwidth. Broadcom's competing Thor Ultra 800G NIC supports up to 8-plane multipathing, enhanced RoCE and programmable congestion control, demonstrating ~96%+ line-rate collective bandwidth.
网络：Nvidia的Spectrum-X多平面架构将每个1.6T NIC分散到八个交换机，声称64倍更高扩展（8K -> 512K Rubin GPU）、1.7倍更少交换机和更好故障恢复能力，BlueField-4成为标准Vera Rubin基础设施处理器，具有800G以太网/7Tbps聚合平台带宽。Broadcom竞争产品Thor Ultra 800G NIC支持最多8平面多路径、增强RoCE和可编程拥塞控制，展示约96%+线速集合带宽。
