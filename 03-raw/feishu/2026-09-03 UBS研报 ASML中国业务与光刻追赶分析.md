3) Future: When will China catch up on lithography?

未来：中国何时能在光刻技术上赶上？
英文原文：
Our base case continues to be that China won't achieve an EUV tool within the next 10 years; looking at patents, they seem to be at a similar stage to ASML in 2004. We believe SMEE and Yuliangsheng are particularly important to watch with the most advanced DUV solutions. On the DUV side, we expect another 2-5 years before China can be competitive, especially on immersion, but believe China will continue to buy ASML tools due to yield and throughput differences. The bear case remains around new restrictions on DUV sales. We estimate China to represent 15-20% of group sales in 2027E.
中文翻译：
