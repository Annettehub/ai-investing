Dear Partners,

We let you down this month. We came closer to permanent capital impairment than is acceptable to us.

While we ultimately found a solution that protected the fund and you as investors, our intention in running the fund is to never find ourselves in such a position in the first place.

Volatility is the price of long-term investment returns. Over the past two years, we have delivered outstanding results, despite occasional sharp pullbacks. But our fund must always be structured such that we can take a loss and fight another day.

I will make it my mission to ensure that we learn the necessary lessons from this experience.

Here is where things stand:

• The portfolio experienced a significant drawdown over the course of July, which was exacerbated by extreme moves in core positions over the past week. Many AI names drew down by half or more, while our positive L/S spread reversed violently. While we could say much about how unusual the month was, we hold ourselves to a higher standard, irrespective of market conditions.

• As these moves proceeded, we started to see increasingly adverse trading in names publicly associated with us. These dynamics are essentially similar to a bank run: vulnerability begetting more vulnerability. We worked to keep the portfolio within our risk parameters, but gradually this became more difficult as positions rapidly moved against us and market liquidity dried up.

• On Wednesday night/Thursday morning, we took decisive action to protect LP capital. We traded a portion of our public portfolio in a block transaction to remove all leverage from the fund, and prevent further losses. All shorts were closed and reliance on portfolio financing removed. We currently manage a fully-paid-for public book (long stock and long fully-paid-for options, with no margin/liquidation risk). This restored stability and allowed us to preserve our private positions.

I take full responsibility for these events.

To be clear, this should rightly have been a very painful month in terms of the performance of our fund: when AI stocks draw down dramatically while AI technical/ business fundamentals are improving, you should expect our fund to be down a lot. We embrace volatility. But it should never jeopardize the fund.

The fund was not shut down, liquidated, or transformed into a private-only fund.
We are continuing to operate as a hybrid public-private fund as before. However, we will manage our public book on a fully-paid-for basis while we draw the lessons from these developments. Most importantly: we took the steps that were necessary to fight another day.

In the coming weeks, I will focus on putting in motion the necessary changes—across our portfolio management, risk team, and vigilance applied across the board—to ensure a higher level of resilience going forward. AI may continue to intensify market volatility for years to come.

These were very expensive scars, but I am dedicated to ensuring they will be invaluable lessons for our organization and for myself as we move forward. My core promise to you is that we will not waste the opportunity to learn from these events.

On the portfolio itself: we are very optimistic about the current investment opportunity set. The underlying fundamentals are accelerating at the very same time that prices have declined significantly.

Thank you for your patience and your partnership. I am fully invested alongside you—virtually all of my capital is in the fund—and I intend to work relentlessly to demonstrate that the events of this month have made me a wiser and stronger investor. I am available next week for 1:1 phone calls with each of you to discuss all of the above.

As an interim update, our current, unaudited estimate of net MTD performance is -67%, and of net YTD performance is +80%. Final figures will follow through our normal reporting process.

We will follow up regarding a group call next week to share further details.

All the best,
Leopold

各位合伙人：

本月我们让你们失望了。我们距离永久性资本减值的距离，已超出我们可接受的范围。

虽然我们最终找到了保护基金以及你们作为投资者的利益的解决方案，但我们管理基金的初衷，是绝不让自己陷入这样的境地。

波动性是长期投资回报的代价。过去两年，尽管偶尔出现大幅回撤，我们依然取得了出色的业绩。但我们的基金必须始终保持这样的结构：即使遭受亏损，也能保存实力、来日再战。

我将把确保我们从这次经历中汲取必要教训作为我的使命。

以下是当前的情况：

• 投资组合在7月份经历了显著回撤，过去一周核心持仓的极端波动进一步加剧了跌势。许多AI相关个股跌幅达一半甚至更多，而我们原本正向的多空价差也剧烈反转。尽管我们可以说这个月有多么不寻常，但无论市场状况如何，我们对自己都秉持更高的标准。

• 随着这些波动持续，我们开始看到与我们有公开关联的个股出现了越来越不利的交易走势。这种动态本质上类似于银行挤兑：脆弱性引发更多的脆弱性。我们努力将投资组合控制在风险参数之内，但随着持仓迅速朝对我们不利的方向变动、市场流动性枯竭，这变得越来越困难。

• 周三晚间/周四凌晨，我们采取了果断行动以保护有限合伙人（LP）资本。我们通过大宗交易处置了部分公开持仓，以移除基金的所有杠杆并防止进一步亏损。所有空头头寸均已平仓，对投资组合融资的依赖也已消除。目前我们管理的是一个全额自有的公开持仓（做多股票及全额支付期权，无保证金/强平风险）。这恢复了稳定性，并使我们得以保留私募持仓。

我对这些事件承担全部责任。

需要明确的是，就基金业绩而言，本月理应是非常痛苦的一个月：当AI股票在AI技术/商业基本面持续改善的同时却大幅下跌时，你们理应预期我们的基金会大幅回撤。我们接受波动性。但它绝不应危及基金本身。

基金并未被关闭、清算或转型为纯私募基金。
我们将继续像以前一样，以混合公开-私募基金的形态运作。然而，在我们从这次事件中吸取教训期间，我们将以全额自有（无杠杆）的方式管理公开持仓。最重要的是：我们采取了必要措施，以确保来日再战。

在接下来的几周里，我将着力推动必要的变革——涵盖投资组合管理、风控团队以及全公司范围的审慎监督——以确保未来具备更高水平的韧性。AI可能会在未来数年内继续加剧市场波动性。

这些是非常昂贵的伤疤，但我致力于确保它们成为我们机构以及我个人前进道路上宝贵的教训。我对你们的核心承诺是：我们不会浪费从这次事件中学习的机会。

关于投资组合本身：我们对当前的投资机会集非常乐观。标的的基本面正在加速改善，而与此同时价格却大幅下跌。

感谢你们的耐心与伙伴关系。我与你们完全利益一致——我几乎所有的资本都在这只基金中——我将不懈努力，以证明本月的事件让我成为一位更明智、更强大的投资者。下周我随时可以与你们中的每一位进行一对一电话沟通，讨论上述所有内容。

作为临时更新，我们目前未经审计的估算净月初至今（MTD）业绩为 -67%，净年初至今（YTD）业绩为 +80%。最终数据将通过我们的正常报告流程公布。

我们将于下周安排一次集体电话会议，以分享更多细节。

此致，
Leopold
