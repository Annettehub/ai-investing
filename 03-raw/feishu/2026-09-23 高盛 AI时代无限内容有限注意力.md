4. 新格式涌现：互动微短剧、超低延迟视频基础设施、虚拟主播/互动直播电商、个性化1对1虚拟伴侣、Harness/Agent编排层

Infinite content, finite attention in the age of AI（AI时代：无限内容，有限注意力）



概述：内容供给无限，注意力有限，行业利润池将被重塑

The entertainment industry is now entering a new era – content supply is becoming almost infinite, generation cost and turnaround have significantly reduced, and interestingly audiences are also getting more used to AI generated content as the quality is improving. In contrast, users' attention is still finite in the age of AI. Hence, we believe this could have a fundamental impact on the China Media and Entertainment industry, and likely shift the industry value chain and profit pool over the next few years.
娱乐行业正进入一个新时代——内容供给正变得几乎无限，生成成本和周转周期显著缩短，有趣的是，随着质量提升，受众也越来越习惯AI生成内容。相比之下，在AI时代用户的注意力仍然有限。因此，我们认为这可能对中国媒体娱乐行业产生根本性影响，并可能在未来几年重塑行业价值链和利润池。
Content production and editing in themselves could become more competitive and commoditized with the help of AI, while long-lasting IP franchise and creative idea generation could become more valuable vs. long-tail content. Content distribution platforms (such as short/mid form video) will benefit from the amplified content supply but also need to balance between real human vibes and the influx of AIGC content.
在AI帮助下，内容制作和编辑本身可能变得更具竞争力和商品化，而长寿IP特许经营和创意想法生成相比长尾内容可能变得更有价值。内容分发平台（如短/中视频）将受益于放大的内容供给，但也需要在真实人类感和AIGC内容涌入之间取得平衡。
We examine the latest AI generation status, cost/time savings, industry value chain impact, potential new business models and associated risks in this report. With average production cost/time savings of 80%-90%+, we would expect more new formats such as interactive cinema/games, infinite dramas and virtual companions to emerge and quickly gain traction.
我们在本报告中考察最新AI生成状态、成本/时间节约、行业价值链影响、潜在新商业模式及相关风险。随着平均制作成本/时间节约80%-90%以上，我们预计更多新格式如互动影院/游戏、无限剧集和虚拟伴侣将涌现并快速获得吸引力。
Among the existing verticals, we believe online gaming remains the most resilient vertical. AI in gaming has tended to aid established publishers more as they have greater potential to leverage the tools to extend their existing IP franchises and evergreen titles. Conversely, there appears to be less headroom for new gaming IP success, a trend observed throughout most of 2026, as long-lasting legacy titles continue to gain share. Long form video and music platforms are seeing substantial cost reduction, but need to manage the conflict between AI content and copyright/catalog content in the longer run in our view.
在现有垂直领域中，我们认为在线游戏仍是最具韧性的垂直领域。游戏中的AI往往更多帮助成熟发行商，因为它们有更大潜力利用工具扩展现有IP特许经营和长青游戏。相反，新游戏IP成功的空间似乎较小，这一趋势在2026年大部分时间都有观察，因为长寿老游戏继续获得份额。长视频和音乐平台看到大幅降本，但我们认为长期需要管理AI内容与版权/目录内容之间的冲突。
For key players, we see Tencent (Buy) as the front-runner in adopting AI initiatives for game development and user experience. NetEase (Buy, on CL) heavily focuses on AI-native gameplay mechanics, LLM-powered autonomous NPCs, generative user-generated content (AI-UGC). See our separate note assessing its 2027 pipeline. We also turn more constructive on XD (see our upgrade to Buy for details), given its Taptap platform will be a beneficiary of abundant game supply, and we believe the stock's narrative will shift from an 'AI loser' in 1H26 (due to higher AI token costs and Taptap Maker's dilution of advertising traffic), to an 'AI beneficiary' given its unique game creation and distribution platform, especially for long-tail game developers.
对于主要参与者，我们认为腾讯（Buy）是在游戏开发和用户体验中采用AI举措的领跑者。网易（Buy，在CL上）重点关注AI原生游戏机制、LLM驱动的自主NPC、生成式用户生成内容（AI-UGC）。参见我们评估其2027年产品管线的单独报告。我们也对XD转为更建设性（详见我们上调至Buy的报告），因为其TapTap平台将受益于丰富的游戏供给，我们认为该股叙事将从1H26的"AI输家"（因更高AI token成本和TapTap Maker稀释广告流量）转向"AI受益者"，鉴于其独特的游戏创作和分发平台，特别是对长尾游戏开发者而言。


What's the current status?（当前状态如何？）



无限视频供给：AI多模态快速发展，短剧上线量同比13倍，游戏8倍以上

Unlimited video supply: over the past few months we have seen rapid evolution in the AI multi-modal space, with the launch of H3 max (from Minimax/Fal), the speed of video generation is now faster than human viewership (generates a 5s video within 3s). In 8M2026, short/mini dramas have seen 13X expansion in terms of launches vs. 2025, while games (including mini-games) have multiplied by more than 8X.
无限视频供给：过去几个月我们看到AI多模态领域快速发展，随着H3 max（来自Minimax/Fal）推出，视频生成速度现在快于人类观看速度（3秒内生成5秒视频）。在2026年8月，短/迷你剧上线量较2025年增长13倍，而游戏（包括迷你游戏）增长了8倍以上。
Higher quality AI content emerging: the proliferation of AI content has now occupied the short drama market over the past 6-12 months (Hongguo 50%+ AI content) and we have now started to see more mid to long AI dramas emerging over reputable platforms like Mango/iQiyi (15-60mins per episode). Lately, the launch of new AI models has also appeared to speed up the process for game development especially mini-games, as 3D/2D assets generation becomes much easier.
更高质量AI内容涌现：过去6-12个月AI内容扩散已占据短剧市场（红果50%+ AI内容），我们现在开始看到更多中长AI剧在芒果/爱奇艺等知名平台上涌现（每集15-60分钟）。最近，新AI模型的推出似乎也加速了游戏开发流程，特别是迷你游戏，因为3D/2D资产生成变得更加容易。
Audience adoption curve also quickly rising: we see the user acceptance of AI content is rising as the quality is improving. This is particularly the case for content where part of the audience scrutinizes quality less, such as in short dramas, music streaming, or swiping short form videos. On the largest drama platforms like Hongguo, the data shows that more than 90% of dramas are now AI generated (animation and human-like).
受众接受度曲线也快速上升：我们看到随着质量提升，用户对AI内容的接受度正在上升。对于部分受众较少审视质量的内容尤其如此，如短剧、音乐流媒体或滑动短视频。在红果等最大短剧平台上，数据显示超过90%的剧集现在是AI生成的（动画和类人）。


How different is AIGC vs. traditional production cost and turnaround?（AIGC与传统制作成本和周期有何不同？）



视频降本80-95%，互动格式降本30-70%，生产周期快5-10倍

The AI generated content production process and cost curve vary in magnitude vs. traditional production, but on average represent a significant cost reduction and much faster turnaround.
AI生成内容制作流程和成本曲线与传统制作相比在幅度上有所不同，但平均而言代表显著的成本降低和更快的周转。
By vertical, we estimate AI video could reduce costs by 80-95% for animation production, music, TVC ads and short form drama, even considering the success rate of AI video generation. For complicated interactive formats such as gaming or long form drama, we estimate at least 30-70% of costs could be reduced, spanning 3D/2D arts generation, graphics/character design etc. For IQ/Mango's recent AIGC long episode, costs reduction is multiple times vs. similar revenue scale dramas in the past.
按垂直领域，我们估计AI视频可为动画制作、音乐、TVC广告和短剧降低80-95%成本，即使考虑AI视频生成成功率。对于游戏或长剧等复杂互动格式，我们估计至少30-70%成本可降低，涵盖3D/2D美术生成、图形/角色设计等。对于爱奇艺/芒果最近的AIGC长剧集，成本降低是过去类似收入规模剧集的数倍。
More importantly, we see the production turnaround is at least 5X-10X faster. Content creators can test ideas at a fraction of costs. In the case of mini-games, a team of 2-3 people can now develop a game within a week, compared to a 3-6 month timeline in the past.
更重要的是，我们看到生产周转至少快5-10倍。内容创作者可以以极低成本测试创意。以迷你游戏为例，2-3人团队现在可以在一周内开发一款游戏，而过去需要3-6个月。
This is also changing how content is regulated. Mango's Beyond Wukong (后西游记) was the first batch of dramas that adopted simultaneous Auditing and Broadcasting modes, speeding up turnaround times.
这也在改变内容监管方式。芒果的《后西游记》是首批采用审播同步模式的剧集之一，加快了周转时间。


How would the value shift alongside the value chain?（价值链价值将如何转移？）



IP所有者影响混合，制作层价值缩水，分发平台分化，AI模型捕获增量价值

Despite the massive cost and time savings, we do not expect all parties to benefit from this shift. How the incremental dollar saved flows is a key debate. Along the value chain:
尽管成本和时间大幅节约，我们并不期望所有方都受益于这一转变。节约的增量美元如何流动是关键争论。沿价值链：
IP owners/developers: mixed impacts - It's likely that top tier and long-lasting IP franchises will still have strong user attention and AI could potentially benefit them by extending their IP lifecycle. However, as countless content is generated, we see the long tail of IPs could face risks differentiating and it is becoming more difficult for new IPs to gain traction in an era where attention is getting more and more fragmented. In the gaming space, the strength of evergreen titles (HoK, FWJ etc.) vs. a lack of new game title success in 2026 echoes our view.
IP所有者/开发者：混合影响——顶级和长寿IP特许经营可能仍拥有强大用户注意力，AI可能通过延长其IP生命周期而使它们受益。然而，随着无数内容被生成，我们看到长尾IP面临差异化风险，在注意力越来越分散的时代，新IP变得越来越难获得吸引力。在游戏领域，长青游戏（王者荣耀、无畏契约等）的强势 vs. 2026年新游戏缺乏成功印证了我们的观点。
Production/implementation layer value would shrink - Convenience and fast content turnaround times of AI tools have reduced the production lifecycle and diluted traditional value add in the past 6 months, as similar content floods the market at unprecedented speed. Competition is becoming more commoditized, unless the content can transition into an enduring IP franchise backed by robust copyright protection. We see physical studios, actors, outsourcing agencies, and graphic design will be the most impacted.
制作/实施层价值将缩水——AI工具的便利性和快速内容周转在过去6个月缩短了制作生命周期并稀释了传统增值，因为类似内容以前所未有的速度涌入市场。竞争正变得更加商品化，除非内容能转化为有强大版权保护支撑的持久IP特许经营。我们认为实体工作室、演员、外包机构和平面设计将受最大影响。
Content distribution platform: overall positive but also diverging - Broadly speaking, in our view the content platforms should benefit from proliferating AI content, as capturing user attention is becoming more difficult in an era of unlimited content supply. In the case of short dramas, despite the massive production cost reduction, most drama producers are not making profits as they spend more to purchase traffic from platforms (like Hongguo or Douyin), and the shelf life of content gets shorter when identical content is produced quicker.
内容分发平台：整体正面但也分化——广义而言，我们认为内容平台应受益于AI内容扩散，因为在无限内容供给时代，捕捉用户注意力变得更加困难。以短剧为例，尽管制作成本大幅降低，大多数短剧制作方并未盈利，因为它们花费更多从平台（如红果或抖音）购买流量，而相同内容被更快生产时，内容的货架期变得更短。
Conversely, platforms whose moat was built on licenses/copyrighted content, we see the barriers to entry diminishing, especially in China where copyright protection remains relatively weak. Music streaming and long form video streaming where the core strategy has been on investing and establishing a content library, will likely experience a greater user preference shift. Moreover, we see potential emergence of new AI-native content platforms, which creates a fresh entertainment experience vs. in the past (such as FAL.live).
相反，对于护城河建立在授权/版权内容上的平台，我们看到进入壁垒正在降低，特别是在中国版权保护仍相对薄弱的情况下。音乐流媒体和长视频流媒体的核心战略一直是投资和建立内容库，可能会经历更大的用户偏好转移。此外，我们看到新的AI原生内容平台可能涌现，创造与过去不同的新鲜娱乐体验（如FAL.live）。
Multi-modal AI models/agents capturing incremental value: The vast majority of AI content is generated via AI video models and harnessing agents. We have seen both AI video models (Seedance/Minimax H3 etc.) and video agents from existing players such as iQiyi/Mango trying to capture the value of this layer. While TAM is growing massively, competition will remain intense on model intelligence/cost efficiency. We see the combination of workflow data, user preferences, creative feedback, proprietary assets, and integrated distribution as key to monitor across different models.
多模态AI模型/agent捕获增量价值：绝大多数AI内容通过AI视频模型和harnessing agents生成。我们看到AI视频模型（Seedance/Minimax H3等）和来自爱奇艺/芒果等现有参与者的视频agent都在尝试捕获这一层的价值。虽然TAM正在大规模增长，但模型智能/成本效率方面的竞争将保持激烈。我们看到工作流数据、用户偏好、创意反馈、专有资产和整合分发的组合是跨不同模型需要监控的关键。


What are the new formats emerging through AI videos?（AI视频催生哪些新格式？）



五大新格式：互动短剧、超低延迟视频基础设施、虚拟主播、虚拟伴侣、Agent编排层

As the marginal cost and latency of video creation have reduced significantly, we see this structural paradigm shift could transition the digital entertainment industry from a scarcity-driven studio model to an infinite, dynamic generation model. Some potential new business models are now emerging, although the way to monetize is still dynamically evolving, we see robust fundamental demand across consumers, professionals and enterprises.
随着视频创作的边际成本和延迟大幅降低，我们看到这一结构性范式转变可能将数字娱乐行业从稀缺驱动的工作室模式转变为无限、动态生成模式。一些潜在新商业模式正在涌现，尽管变现方式仍在动态发展，我们看到消费者、专业人士和企业都有强劲的基本面需求。
1. Dynamic & Interactive Micro-Dramas (互动短剧): The explosion of mobile-first serialized micro-dramas is merging with real-time video generation to replace static video with interactive, generative narrative experiences. Rather than pre-rendering hundreds of story branches in a studio, the narrative is generated dynamically based on real-time viewer choices or sentiment analysis.
1. 动态与互动微短剧：移动优先的连载微短剧爆炸式增长正在与实时视频生成融合，以互动、生成式叙事体验替代静态视频。叙事不是在工作室预渲染数百个故事分支，而是根据实时观众选择或情感分析动态生成。
2. Ultra-Low Latency Video Infrastructure: As inference speeds achieve sub-second and real-time generation speeds, streaming workflows are moving to live video transformation. Real-Time Video-to-Video Streaming Pipelines: Platforms like fal.live enable developers to run serverless, distributed ComfyUI workflows and video diffusion pipelines with minimal latency. This also enables multi-user live storyboards where creative teams, game designers, or audiences co-direct visual environments in real time.
2. 超低延迟视频基础设施：随着推理速度达到亚秒级和实时生成速度，流媒体工作流正在转向实时视频转换。实时视频到视频流媒体管线：fal.live等平台使开发者能够以最小延迟运行无服务器、分布式ComfyUI工作流和视频扩散管线。这也支持多用户实时故事板，创意团队、游戏设计师或观众实时共同导演视觉环境。
3. Virtual Streamers & Interactive Livestream Commerce: Virtual idols and AI live streamers are evolving from simple animated 2D/3D avatars driven by human motion-capture to fully autonomous, multi-modal agents capable of 24/7 broadcast operations. Autonomous Commerce Livestreaming Hosts: Generative agents trained on product catalogs and sales scripts that interact in real time with viewer comments, adjust pacing, showcase dynamically rendered products, and run continuous sales loops.
3. 虚拟主播与互动直播电商：虚拟偶像和AI主播正在从由人类动作捕捉驱动的简单动画2D/3D头像演进为能够24/7播出运营的完全自主多模态agent。自主商务直播主播：在产品目录和销售脚本上训练的生成式agent，实时与观众评论互动、调整节奏、展示动态渲染产品并运行连续销售循环。
4. Personalized 1-on-1 Virtual Companions: High-fidelity virtual characters offering private or small-group interactive video sessions for entertainment, coaching, or fan engagement. Monetization is straightforward on time-based billing (e.g., $1/minute video sessions) and subscription tiers for persistent episodic memory.
4. 个性化1对1虚拟伴侣：高保真虚拟角色提供私人或小组互动视频会话，用于娱乐、教练或粉丝互动。变现方式直接，按时长计费（如$1/分钟视频会话）和订阅层级以获得持久剧集记忆。
5. The Harness & Agent Orchestration Layer: we see the "Harness Layer"—platforms that assemble multi-agent workflows, long-form narrative consistency, asset management, and studio pipelines could also capture higher economic value over time.
5. Harness与Agent编排层：我们看到"Harness层"——组装多agent工作流、长篇叙事一致性、资产管理和工作室管线的平台，随着时间推移也可能捕获更高经济价值。
Full-Stack Cinematic Production Agents (e.g iQIYI's Nadou Pro): Model: Enterprise and prosumer SaaS + API token margins. Nadou Pro coordinates specialized sub-agents (scriptwriting, shot composition, camera direction, audio, and visual effects) across multiple underlying foundation models (QiZhi, Seedance, H3) to solve narrative and stylistic consistency across long-form projects. Revenue Capture: Tiered enterprise licensing, compute margin markups, and end-to-end profit-sharing on syndicated distributions.
全栈电影制作Agent（如爱奇艺的Nadou Pro）：模式：企业和专业消费者SaaS + API token利润。Nadou Pro协调专业子agent（编剧、镜头构图、导演、音频和视觉效果）跨多个基础模型（奇艺、Seedance、H3），解决长篇项目的叙事和风格一致性。收入捕获：分层企业许可、计算利润加价和联合发行的端到端利润分成。
Creator Model Hubs & Workflow Marketplaces (e.g., Liblib AI, TapMaker, Updream): Model: Platforms that blend community-driven style asset distribution with cloud inference. Liblib monetizes by acting as a unified studio interface where creators license visual assets and train proprietary workflows. Revenue Capture: Compute arbitrage margins, membership subscription, and marketplace take-rates on creator-published workflow nodes and character IP.
创作者模型中心与工作流市场（如Liblib AI、TapMaker、Updream）：模式：融合社区驱动风格资产分发与云推理的平台。Liblib通过充当统一工作室界面变现，创作者在此许可视觉资产并训练专有工作流。收入捕获：计算套利利润、会员订阅和创作者发布的工作流节点及角色IP的市场抽成。


What are the potential challenges ahead?（未来潜在挑战是什么？）



两大挑战：变现（计算成本高但消费者付费意愿有限）和监管

We think the core debate here is whether AI products can generate durable user value, monetize at margins above inference and distribution costs, and operate safely within an increasingly complex legal environment?
我们认为核心争论在于AI产品能否产生持久用户价值、以高于推理和分发成本的利润率变现，并在日益复杂的法律环境中安全运营？
Monetization: incremental compute cost is still high but consumer paying propensity remains limited. When users can create unlimited images, videos, characters, stories, and ads at near-zero marginal creative cost, consumers may enjoy creating content but still be reluctant to pay recurring subscriptions. For entertainment products, unlimited supply may also create content fatigue. More AI dramas, virtual idols, and interactive stories do not automatically translate into more viewing time or higher user spending.
