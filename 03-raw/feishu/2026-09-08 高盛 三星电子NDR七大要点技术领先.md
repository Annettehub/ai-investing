7 Key NDR Takeaways（NDR七大要点）


1. 2027年内存供应更紧张，紧张持续到2028年



要点一：内存供需失衡将在2027年扩大

EN: Reiterated its view of 2027 memory supply being tighter than 2026, with tightness to persist in 2028. With the memory demand fulfillment rate being at only around a 60% level, but demand continuing to grow while production growth is limited in the near-term, SEC reiterated its view that it expects the supply and demand imbalance for memory to widen in 2027 compared to this year, and for tightness to persist even into 2028 even if all the announced industry capacity comes online.

中文：重申其观点，即2027年内存供应比2026年更紧张，紧张将持续到2028年。由于内存需求满足率仅约60%水平，而需求持续增长、近期产量增长有限，三星重申其预期：与今年相比，2027年内存供需失衡将扩大，即使所有已宣布的行业产能上线，紧张也将持续到2028年。


2. Shell-first策略：灵活性与警惕过度投资



要点二：建设外壳保持灵活，设备投资基于确认需求

EN: Shell-first strategy to offer better flexibility, while remaining vigilant on potential over-investment: SEC has continued to maintain a high level of capex even during the downcycle, which has led to a higher clean room capacity for the company relative to its peers during the current cycle. The company plans to maintain this shell-first strategy which would enable enhanced flexibility in meeting customers' demand, while its cautiously optimistic view on the cycle should help prevent any potential over-investment. In other words, while the company continues to build the fab shells to prepare for future demand, the company's investment on equipment for actual production will be based on confirmed demand, not ahead of demand.

中文：Shell-first策略提供更好的灵活性，同时对潜在过度投资保持警惕：三星即使在下行周期也持续维持高水平资本开支，这使其在当前周期中相对于同行拥有更高的洁净室产能。公司计划维持这一shell-first策略，以增强满足客户需求的灵活性，同时其对周期的谨慎乐观看法应有助于防止任何潜在的过度投资。换言之，虽然公司继续建设晶圆厂外壳为未来需求做准备，但公司对实际生产设备的投资将基于已确认的需求，而非超前于需求。


3. LTA长期协议进展顺利



要点三：LTA占产能目标60-70%，降低行业周期性

EN: LTA discussions progressing well; better visibility and more efficient investment decisions compared to past cycles: At its 2Q26 earnings call, SEC disclosed that it has completed long-term agreements (LTA) with the top five global data center customers, and is in the final stage of negotiations with five additional large customers related to AI demand. During the NDR, the company highlighted that these five additional negotiations are progressing well, and customers continue to come back wanting to add even additional years beyond the typical 5-year LTAs. SEC also stressed that its target of LTAs eventually accounting for 60-70% of its planned production capacity would provide them with more control and flexibility with securing better visibility on its long-term revenue and earnings. This improved demand visibility would also lead to more efficient investment decisions for the company, helping to reduce the cyclicality in the memory industry that we have seen in the past.

中文：LTA讨论进展顺利，与过去周期相比有更好的可见性和更高效的投资决策：在2Q26财报电话会上，三星披露已与全球前五大数据中心客户完成长期协议（LTA），并正与另外五家与AI需求相关的大客户进行最后阶段谈判。在NDR期间，公司强调这五家额外谈判进展顺利，客户不断回来希望在典型的5年LTA之外再增加更多年份。三星还强调，其LTA最终占计划产能60-70%的目标将为其提供更多控制和灵活性，确保更好的长期收入和盈利可见性。这种改善的需求可见性也将带来更高效的投资决策，有助于降低我们过去看到的内存行业周期性。


4. 技术领先是优先事项



要点四：研发占收入超10%，HBM4/HBM4E技术领先

EN: Technology leadership the priority: Throughout the meetings, SEC emphasized how it is prioritizing technology leadership. The company has continued to spend a significant amount on R&D, where it saw a 26%/78% yoy growth in R&D expense in 1Q26/2Q26, and the R&D expense as % of its revenue has exceeded 10% level during the past 3 years. The company is also running a fab designated for R&D which it completed at the end of 2024. SEC believes that technology leadership is the main factor in attracting customers, HBM4 being one of the recent examples, with the company expecting its industry leading D1c for core die and 4nm base die to contribute to significant growth in its HBM business. In addition to the tech leadership in HBM4, the company has shipped the industry's first 12-layer HBM4E samples to global customers in May, and has recently provided its AI memory technology roadmap including preview of its zHBM and zNAND-O concept models, displaying its focus on technology leadership.

中文：技术领先是优先事项：在整个会议中，三星强调其如何优先考虑技术领先。公司持续投入大量研发费用，1Q26/2Q26研发费用同比增长26%/78%，过去3年研发费用占收入比例已超过10%。公司还运营着一座2024年底建成的专用研发晶圆厂。三星认为技术领先是吸引客户的主要因素，HBM4就是最近的例子之一，公司预计其行业领先的D1c核心die和4nm base die将为HBM业务带来显著增长。除HBM4的技术领先外，公司已于5月向全球客户出货了行业首款12层HBM4E样品，并最近公布了其AI内存技术路线图，包括zHBM和zNAND-O概念模型的预览，展示了其对技术领先的专注。


5. 代工业务步入正轨



要点五：先进制程接近满产，HBM4 base die需求强劲

EN: Foundry business on track, with strong demand for advanced node: SEC emphasized that foundry continues to be an important business for the company. While the foundry business profit breakeven may need some more time given the provisioning for a special bonus and Taylor fab depreciation starting to kick in when production begins soon, the company commented that the business is very much on track especially with its advanced nodes at close to full utilization with tailwinds including strong demand for HBM4 base die.

中文：代工业务步入正轨，先进制程需求强劲：三星强调代工仍然是公司的重要业务。虽然鉴于特别奖金计提和Taylor晶圆厂折旧将在不久后生产开始时启动，代工业务盈利盈亏平衡可能还需要一些时间，但公司表示该业务非常步入正轨，特别是其先进制程接近满产，利好因素包括HBM4 base die的强劲需求。


6. 定制化需求上升，内存+代工协同



要点六：唯一同时做内存和代工的公司，协同效应放大

EN: Rising needs for customization boding well with its memory and foundry capabilities: SEC mentioned that as customization is becoming more important and difficult, the company's capabilities and synergy in being the only company that can do both memory and foundry is starting to get more appreciated from customers as one of its core competitive strengths. SEC believes that as HBM becomes more customized heading into the next generation of products such as HBM4E and HBM5, the synergy between its memory and foundry businesses will likely amplify and act as one of its key competitive advantages.

中文：定制化需求上升，利好其内存和代工能力：三星提到，随着定制化变得越来越重要和困难，公司作为唯一同时能做内存和代工的公司，其能力和协同效应开始得到客户更多认可，成为其核心竞争优势之一。三星认为，随着HBM进入HBM4E和HBM5等下一代产品变得更加定制化，其内存和代工业务之间的协同效应可能会放大，并成为其关键竞争优势之一。


7. 股东回报：回购与分红均考虑



要点七：已提前支付2024-2026累计FCF的50%，未来考虑所有选项

EN: All options including both buybacks and dividends considered for its shareholder return: SEC commented that it provided an early communication of the company's commitment in paying 50% of the cumulative FCF between 2024 to 2026, and an early dividend payment unlike in the past where it announced the return at the end of the policy period. The company is considering all options including buybacks and dividends for its future shareholder return, and the ultimate consideration would be how to maximize its shareholders' benefit, not necessarily any implications that its decisions would have to its Group affiliate companies.

中文：股东回报考虑所有选项，包括回购和分红：三星表示，公司提前传达了支付2024至2026年累计自由现金流50%的承诺，并提前支付了股息——不同于过去在政策期结束时才宣布回报。公司正在考虑未来股东回报的所有选项，包括回购和分红，最终考量将是如何最大化股东利益，而不一定是其决策对集团关联公司的影响。

投资逻辑



Investment Thesis

EN: Samsung Electronics (SEC) is one of the largest tech companies in the world, with the #1 market share in several products including memory chips, OLED panels, smartphones, and TVs. We expect the strong earnings power led by memory to continue, supported by our expectation of solid memory pricing continuing and also remaining at an elevated level. We expect this to be helped by the long-term agreements (LTA) with customers, with a higher level of binding commitment likely this time compared to the past. The company is starting to show meaningful progress in HBM, and also considering the higher scope for meaningful shareholder returns, we remain constructive on the name and reiterate our Buy rating.

中文：三星电子（SEC）是全球最大的科技公司之一，在内存芯片、OLED面板、智能手机和电视等多个产品中拥有第一的市场份额。我们预计内存带动的强劲盈利能力将持续，支撑因素是我们预期内存价格将继续坚挺并维持在高位。我们预计这将得益于与客户的长期协议（LTA），此次的约束承诺水平可能高于过去。公司在HBM方面开始显示出有意义的进展，同时考虑到有意义的股东回报空间更大，我们对该股保持建设性看法并重申买入评级。
