一、HPC计算驱动整体制造创新

HPC computing drives holistic manufacturing innovation
HPC计算驱动整体制造创新
SEMICON Taiwan kicked off on August 31st with IC Forum and Silicon Photonics Summit, both featuring TSMC's presentations. At the session in IC Forum, TSMC shared its view that the data center power capacity will accelerate from 5-6GW per year from '15-24 to 30-40GW per year from '25-30, reaching 300GW installed base by '30 as AI training scales toward multimodal (5x per annum LLM compute growth). In addition, it sees robust AI inference demand, with token consumption growing ~500x since '22 led by agents and reasoning and further compounding by 24x to 120 quadrillion tokens by '30.
SEMICON Taiwan于8月31日开幕，设有IC论坛和硅光子峰会，两场均有台积电的演讲。在IC论坛环节，台积电分享了其观点：数据中心电力容量将从2015-2024年的每年5-6GW加速到2025-2030年的每年30-40GW，到2030年达到300GW装机容量，因为AI训练向多模态扩展（LLM算力每年增长5倍）。此外，台积电看到强劲的AI推理需求，token消费量自2022年以来增长约500倍（由代理和推理驱动），到2030年将进一步复合增长24倍至120千万亿tokens。
TSMC has AI platform across logic (A13, A12, N2U). Si-technology for HBM scaling (from N12 for HBM4 to N3 for HBM5) and system integration (e.g. SoIC with 11 tape outs for GPU/CPU by 4Q26 and >14x reticle size CoWoS by '29) and compute fabric (e.g. CPO).
台积电拥有覆盖logic (A13, A12, N2U)的AI平台。HBM扩展的硅技术（从N12用于HBM4到N3用于HBM5）和系统集成（例如SoIC到4Q26有11个GPU/CPU tape-out，到'29年CoWoS超过14倍光罩尺寸）和计算架构（例如CPO）。
二、硅光子加速实现更快I/O扩展

Silicon photonics acceleration to enable faster I/O scaling
硅光子加速实现更快I/O扩展
TSMC believes the optical I/O is becoming prerequisite for AI/HPC system as the I/O gap is widening between the compute scaling (3x every 2 years) and I/O scaling (1.4x every two years). Its COUPE platform and a broad photonics device/PDK library facilitate its customer designs to enhance the connectivity through two complementary paths – "faster and narrowing" with higher per lane speed (200Gbps to 400Gbps) and lane count (128+ lanes) and "slower and wider" by increasing wavelengths from 1 to 16+ through WDM.
台积电认为，光I/O正成为AI/HPC系统的先决条件，因为计算扩展（每2年3倍）和I/O扩展（每2年1.4倍）之间的I/O差距正在扩大。其COUPE平台和广泛的光子器件/PDK库通过两条互补路径帮助客户设计增强连接性——"更快更窄"（更高的单通道速度200Gbps→400Gbps和通道数128+通道）和"更慢更宽"（通过WDM将波长从1增加到16+）。
To allow further production ramp and wider adoption of the technology platform, TSMC has lowered COUPE grating coupler insertion loss while improved edge coupler performance and sees wafer level testing is critical to reduce the wasted packaging cost.
为了进一步扩大生产和更广泛采用该技术平台，台积电降低了COUPE光栅耦合器的插入损耗，同时改善了边缘耦合器性能，并认为晶圆级测试对于减少浪费的封装成本至关重要。
